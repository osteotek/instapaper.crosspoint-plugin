# Articles for Instapaper

Read your saved Instapaper articles on the reader as EPUB books.

## Features

- Sync articles from Home, Archive, Liked or your own folders on the reader's web **Settings** page.
- With the optional converter, a free Cloudflare Worker on your own account: images and a cover sized for the reader, browsing and downloading articles on the reader itself, reading progress sent to Instapaper, and archiving the articles you finish.
- Send the highlights you make on the reader to Instapaper.
- Delete articles from the card once they are archived in Instapaper.

## Setup

- Create an application on Instapaper's developer page and generate an access token.
- Open the reader's web **Settings** page and paste the token on the **Articles for Instapaper** card.
- Optional: deploy the converter with one button, then enter its address on the card.

## Notes

- Each user needs their own Instapaper application and token.
- Without Instapaper Premium, Instapaper accepts five highlights a month.
- Not made or endorsed by Instapaper.
- Full guide: github.com/osteotek/instapaper.crosspoint-plugin
